About Me

Hi, I'm Michael.

I'm a videographer, photographer, and editor drawn to movement, light, and the stories that unfold in between.

My work spans real estate, commercial projects, interviews, events, weddings, portraits, and narrative filmmaking. Every project is a chance to see something differently, meet someone new, and create something meaningful.

Beyond the camera, I'm also a salsa instructor, and my curiosity has taken me through martial arts, skiing, rafting, and plenty of other adventures along the way. Each new experience offers a fresh perspective and a new skill to master. That same spirit of exploration shapes my creative work, inspiring me to keep learning, experimenting, and refining my craft.

If you're ready to create something together, I'd love to hear your story and see where it takes us.
