About Me

For over 10 years, I've been capturing people, places, and stories through the lens. My experience spans corporate and commercial productions, documentaries, real estate, live events, weddings, and independent films. Along the way, I've had the opportunity to work on projects for clients including NBC's The Voice, Hilton, Dell, Boeing, and many others. My experience includes filming interviews for Netflix documentaries, managing camera crews for independent films, and producing multi-camera live events.

I work with professional cinema cameras, lighting, audio, and stabilization equipment, including a RED Komodo X and Sony mirrorless cameras. I'm also an FAA Part 107 certified drone pilot, expanding the creative possibilities with aerial photography and cinematography. In post-production, my work includes multi-camera editing, color correction and grading, audio mixing, and delivering polished content across a variety of formats and platforms.

While I have a base rate, every project is unique, and the time, equipment, and creative effort involved can vary quite a bit. I'm always happy to discuss your ideas, understand your needs, and put together a quote that reflects the scope of your project. Feel free to reach out with any questions, big or small. I do my best to respond quickly, and I'd love to hear what you have in mind!
