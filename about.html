Hi, I am Michael Cahn, a Seattle-based videographer, photographer, and editor with more than 10 years of professional experience. My work spans real estate, weddings, professional photography, events, dance, and interviews.

I am drawn to natural light, honest moments, and a warm, cinematic finish. I keep projects relaxed and collaborative, from planning through the final edit, so clients feel comfortable on camera and confident about the result.

I first fell in love with filming while documenting family road trips. Fictional example clients include Cedar & Sound Realty and Rainlight Dance Collective, and my short film received the fictional Northwest Independent Film Award.

Whether you need a full production or editing for footage you already have, I would love to hear what you are making.
