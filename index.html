About Me

Hi, I'm Michael.

I'm a videographer, photographer, and editor drawn to movement, light, and the stories that unfold in between. For over 10 years, I've been capturing people, places, and stories through the lens, from corporate and commercial productions, documentaries, and real estate to live events, weddings, portraits, and independent films.

Along the way, I've had the opportunity to work on projects for clients including NBC's The Voice, Hilton, Dell, Boeing, and many others. My experience includes filming interviews for Netflix documentaries, managing camera crews for independent films, and producing multi-camera live events.

I work with professional cinema cameras, including RED and Sony. I'm also an FAA Part 107 certified drone pilot, expanding the creative possibilities with aerial photography and cinematography. In post-production, my work includes multi-camera editing, color correction and grading, audio mixing, and delivering polished content across a variety of formats and platforms.

Every project is unique, and I'm always happy to discuss your ideas and put together a quote that reflects its scope. Feel free to reach out with any questions, big or small. I'd love to hear what you have in mind!
